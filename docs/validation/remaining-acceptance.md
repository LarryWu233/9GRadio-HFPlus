# Resume checklist after the nightly checkpoint

This records unfinished engineering and acceptance work. The nightly target of approximately 75% is an engineering estimate, not a claim that 75% of every test gate has passed or that the remaining elapsed time is proportional. The branch stays separate from main.

1. **Controlled high-rate CW reproduction.** Preview 07/08 fail the original 1.92 MS/s CW realtime case under current device conditions. Archived preview 06 fails the identical test binary too. Keep all decoding, exact sample-count and 15% timing assertions. Establish device temperature, competing processes and clock conditions, then separate FIR throughput from scheduling/producer pacing. Do not attribute failure to thermal throttling without a controlled result. Only evaluate a mature replacement through the agreed correctness/resource and paired full-path benefit gates.
2. **Finish the compatibility facade.** Extract remaining channel rate/configuration policy and protocol/dual-APRS routing from `DspEngine`. Preserve spectrum/SQL calibration, existing algorithms, public UI behavior and decoder event semantics. Keep stages serial where lightweight; do not add a general graph engine or per-node threads.
3. **Complete component decisions.** PFFFT remains an optional injected adapter; default-size timing did not reach the 20% gate. SpeexDSP/clock control, liquid-dsp and Oboe build on all ABIs but their full-path comparison, callback/lifecycle and production clock integration gates are incomplete. Keep current backends until evidence qualifies a replacement. The successful accelerated drift simulation uses a known clock and is not integrated Android clock validation.
4. **Finish lifetime/fault coverage.** Retain current 300 processing lifecycle cycles, native decoder cycles, input/output congestion and cancellation tests. Add uncovered hardware permission-denial, unplug/reconnect/rate-switch combinations and a full settings/recording/decoder inventory. Check pending resource ownership after faults; cancellation alone is not proof of release.
5. **Run the full stability matrix on the final code.** The earlier WFM and AM 30-minute results belong to their recorded preview versions. Complete HF+ and RTL WFM/AM/SSB long runs on the final implementation, plus NR/NB/tune/rate changes. Stable windows require zero application input loss, PCM drops and underrun increments; record warm-up losses separately. Independently sourced samples are still required for protocols beyond the documented ADS-B fixture.
6. **Measure UI/resources and real playback clock behavior.** Obtain same-device, same-display baseline comparisons; 60 seconds of interaction, slow frames below 5%, no pause over 100 ms, CPU/memory within 10%. Current headless CPU/max-heap figures are not substitutes. Validate integrated monitor-only clock adaptation with ±100 ppm behavior without changing decoder or recording sample timelines.
7. **User Android 16 acceptance.** Deliver the independent preview and [manual checklist](android16-manual.md). Actual 16 KB-page execution remains explicitly pending unless that runtime is exercised. User confirmation is required before merge.
8. **Final delivery and merge.** Update the feature matrix, component decisions, performance evidence and sanitized release assets; remove temporary comparison/test entry points and auxiliary packages. Recheck remote main, rebase if needed and repeat affected checks. Keep PR draft and do not merge until every required gate, including user acceptance, is met.
